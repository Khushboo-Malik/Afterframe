AfterFrame, screen by screen

Every screen of the website, drawn with sample data before any code exists. The trip is a Lisbon vlog with 14 places; the grid has 12 trips.

Click pins and place cards, try the category chips, and switch Desktop / Phone at the top. The site follows your device's light or dark setting. To ask for changes, tell Claude the section letter and what to change, for example “D: make the pins bigger”.

A

Home

Header search bar, the big demo video, then every mapped trip.

  • Search bar takes a link (maps it) or plain text (filters the grid by title).
  • Demo video is a YouTube embed of NEXT_PUBLIC_DEMO_YOUTUBE_ID, loaded on click. Hidden until the demo exists.
  • Grid shows done or partial trips with at least one place, newest first, 24 per page, then Load more.
  • Columns: 4 on desktop, 2 on tablet, 1 on phone. Clicking a card opens the trip page (section D).
B

Search bar: errors and text search

A link that can't be mapped shows a message under the bar. Plain text searches the mapped trips.

Preview:
  • Submitting: the bar is disabled and the button spins until the backend answers.
  • Copy is the backend's message, shown as-is. Red for a problem with the link, blue for limits and “coming soon”.
  • Plain text goes to /?q=lisbon and filters trips by title.
  • Proposed, not in the spec yet: hide the demo video while searching, and the “no matches” message in the third frame.
C

Trip page while mapping

The loading scene covers the map until the first place arrives. Then the map takes over and pins drop in.

  • Stage line follows the real stage: queued → watching → verifying. This mockup cycles through it on a timer.
  • Photos crossfade every 6 s with a slow zoom, and a fact changes every 5 s. With reduced motion, the photos and emojis stay still.
  • After the first place, the map replaces the scene, pins drop in, and a slim bar runs under the header.
  • Question: the “📍 N places found” counter can only read 0, because the scene is replaced as soon as the first place arrives. Here the count carries on in the trip strip instead.
D

Trip page

The trip strip, a full-width map with numbered pins, and the numbered place cards. Pin 3 was clicked, so card 3 is highlighted.

  • Pins: solid means verified, hollow means unconfirmed, colored by category. Numbers follow the video's order. Unmatched places get no number.
  • Click a pin and its card scrolls into view and highlights. Click a card and the map moves to its pin.
  • Chips filter pins and cards together. Numbers never change.
  • “Looks right ✓” is remembered on this device only. Cards show no ratings or photos, because those Places fields are off-limits.
E

Trip page in a phone browser

Trip strip, then the map at about 60% of the screen, then single-column cards. These two frames are always phone-sized.

  • Chips scroll sideways in one row, so the strip stays short.
  • Watch out: central Lisbon pins are a few hundred meters apart and overlap at the fit-all zoom on a phone. Tapping a card zooms the map to its pin.
F

State cards

Partial, never mapped, failed or stalled, and no places found.

  • Partial: the banner sits above the trip, and the places found so far stay usable.
  • Never mapped: shown when the trip isn't in the database (404), using the public YouTube thumbnail.
  • Failed and stalled share one card. Try again submits the link again.
  • No places: the video was watched but nothing travel-related was found.
